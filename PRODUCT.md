# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Business owners and teams evaluating software for customer-facing experiences or internal operations. They need to understand what Rakmyat builds, how a project is approached, and how to start a conversation.

## Product Purpose

Explain Rakmyat's software-house services accurately in English and Arabic and help prospective clients decide whether to make an inquiry. Success means clear service understanding and a trustworthy path to the localized Contact page.

## Brand Personality

Professional, clear, technically credible. The voice is concise, practical, and client-focused. It should evoke confidence through useful explanations rather than unsupported claims.

## Anti-references

- Invented customer outcomes, company history, team credentials, statistics, testimonials, or delivery promises.
- Generic AI marketing language and vague claims.
- Fake product screenshots or invented customer interfaces presented as delivered work.
- Dark or purple redesigns, excessive gradients, generic dashboard mockups, random floating cards, and oversized empty placeholders.
- Incomplete Arabic localization or incorrect RTL behavior.

## Design Principles

1. Preserve the recognizable Iteck light theme, blue accents, illustrations, typography, and responsive composition.
2. Explain services through real business tasks, users, and workflows.
3. Keep English and Arabic equally complete and natural.
4. Use factual, qualified language; distinguish conceptual visuals from real project media.
5. Make the next step clear without implying a form or service promise that is not verified.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Preserve keyboard access and visible focus, use semantic headings and meaningful alternative text, respect reduced motion, and verify Arabic direction and reading order.
