# Known Issues and Production Decisions

- **Brand identity:** the BDA project identifies the source logo as user-supplied and authoritative; its derived mark is now used in the existing Iteck header/footer wordmarks. The original Iteck light palette remains. No approved palette/type token sheet or logo/media license document was found.
- **Business copy:** bilingual positioning and a five-area service catalogue were adapted from owner-provided BDA materials. This establishes the supplied source, not independently verified delivery history or project outcomes. Obtain owner approval of public wording, scope, and any operational claims before production.
- **Portfolio and proof:** all nine BDA-nominated projects remain candidates. Rakmyat attribution, project scope, and screenshot/content publication rights are not established. The Work page describes service capabilities; candidate projects are not exposed as delivered client work. Review the [Phase 3.1 candidate inventory](./phase-3.1/portfolio-candidate-review.json) and [implementation report](./phase-3.1/implementation-report.md).
- **Contact:** BDA materials contain `info@rakmyat.online`, a phone number, and the `rakmyat.online` domain, but these remain source candidates pending confirmation. They are not published. The current form validates in the browser but sends and stores nothing.
- **Domain and SEO:** no production domain is configured. Canonical URLs are omitted and the sitemap has no absolute entries until `NEXT_PUBLIC_SITE_URL` is set.
- **Arabic review:** Arabic routes render RTL and have localized copy. The new Arabic draft needs professional proofreading before production. The pilot Tajawal fonts were not imported because approval and licensing evidence are absent.
- **Visual review:** Edge route checks covered both locales at a 1424×954 CSS viewport with no initial broken images or horizontal overflow. A matched visual diff and the requested 1440, 1280, 768, and 390 viewport passes were not completed; current Edge automation did not expose a viewport override. See the Phase 3.1 report.
- **Functionality:** portfolio filters were exercised. The project-candidate routes remain unpublished/404. The contact form is demo-only. The scope table is informational and not a quote. The workflow panel is explicitly illustrative, not a testimonial or case study.
- **Source and rights:** only an image export of the user-supplied logo mark was copied from the BDA project; candidate portfolio screenshots and pilot assets were not copied. The original theme media and Font Awesome Pro files still need applicable redistribution-rights review. No vendor private source, PHP backend, or CMS is present.
- **Accessibility and production:** no deployment occurred. Manual contrast, screen reader, 200% zoom/reflow, complete cross-browser, animation timing, and final brand/copy review remain open.
