## Deletion workflow proposal

1. Receive a request through the owner-approved privacy contact; do not collect extra sensitive identity documents by default.
2. Verify the requester proportionately using the approved process and record the request ID/date without copying inquiry contents into logs.
3. An authorized administrator locates the inquiry and linked notification row; confirm no legal hold or documented retention exception before deletion.
4. Delete/anonymize the inquiry and cascading notification record using a reviewed database operation. Keep only a minimal non-content audit record if approved.
5. Explain the backup expiry window and let encrypted backups age out under the approved schedule; document any restoration procedure that could reintroduce deleted data.
6. Confirm completion through the approved contact channel and retain the minimal request outcome for the approved period.

**Not implemented or exercised against live records.** The owner/legal reviewer must approve controller identity, lawful basis, rights wording, retention, backup expiry, cross-border providers and response commitments before publication or production data collection.
