# Privacy, assets and copy

The contact form collects name and phone as required fields; email, company, service, budget, timeline, project details and reference URL are optional. Submissions persist in PostgreSQL and are available to authorized administrators; a notification email contains submitted fields and is sent to the configured owner recipient. Database/backup/log hosting and SMTP are potential processors to identify after provider selection.

Before release, the owner/legal reviewer must approve purpose/lawful basis, user-facing privacy notice, data retention period, deletion workflow, access roles, processor terms, cross-border processing, and breach contact. No retention duration or legal approval is inferred here. Implement a documented deletion request process and verify deleted records are removed from active storage and expire from backups per approved retention.

A bilingual privacy notice and deletion workflow draft is in [privacy-notice-draft-en-ar.md](privacy-notice-draft-en-ar.md); bracketed fields are intentionally unresolved and must be completed and reviewed before publication.

Repository presence does not prove publication rights. Evidence remains needed for the Iteck theme, Font Awesome/icons, bundled fonts, third-party font rights, illustrations, project screenshots/media, client names/attribution, and any copied reference material. Review all English/Arabic company/service claims and project descriptions with the owner; verify response-time promises and client attribution. No legal clearance or copy signoff was supplied.
